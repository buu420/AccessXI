# AccessXI v2026.09.05.1

Windurst 1 could replace examining the Magical Gizmo with entering Inner
Horutoto through West Sarutabaruta. That entrance lands in a disconnected
section. The mission now retains the exact Lily Tower gate and East
Sarutabaruta entrance, and its earlier travel step reaches the tower itself.

The shared resolver consults compact action targets before substituting a
zone entrance. Reviewed bindings can identify a particular same-name object;
a missing exact identity cannot silently fall back to a different object.
The two existing reviewed San d'Oria branch contacts now ship in the package.

Entrance selection uses 4,166 native component checks across 75 zones for
the three nation mission catalogues. A measured connected entrance takes
precedence over arbitrary zone-graph choice. All-negative mesh results retain
the destination with an explicit unverified-approach warning: other terrain,
recorded paths, doors and transports may still provide a route. Every final
walking route remains subject to the existing runtime validity checks.

Recovery from the wrong Horutoto component can leave through West Sarutabaruta,
cross East Sarutabaruta and return through Lily Tower. Each walking leg must
pass the normal validator. Both synchronous refusal and asynchronous terrain
failure can invoke recovery; intermediate zone-search legs cannot replace the
original final destination.

## Validation

- 17 focused destination/identity/entrance claims; 14 production source-route
  integration claims, including preservation of the guide approach.
- 551 independent nation entrance consistency claims; 131 existing resolver
  claims. Catalogue census counts are not claims of mission completion.
- Three-crossing recovery, every failed walking leg, invalid zone transitions,
  synchronous/asynchronous dispatch and preservation of zone-search ownership.
- 12 route validity claims; 17 Promyvion obstacle claims; automatic support
  logger, speech, report export and failure integration.
- Stock Lua 5.1 syntax for 188 addon files; 154 module forward-scope checks.
- Installer/package, migration, public hygiene and updater security gates.

The native matrix contains 1,980 connected pairs, 2,173 mesh-no-path pairs and
13 unknown pairs. Independent enumeration finds 139 destination points without
positive connectivity and one uncovered Star Sibyl candidate. The guide audit
still identifies 123 refusals and 166 zone-only substitutes among 710 actionable
steps. These are recorded gaps, not certified working missions.

The native Lily entrance-to-gate route repaired to 78 points with no blind legs.
The exterior recovery corridors connected, but contained residual zero-clearance
points. No retail walkthrough or installer rerun was performed during this repair.

Automatic mission/zone logging remains enabled. `/axi report` exports
`AccessXI-support-report.log`; entrance decisions and unverified approaches now
add explicit breadcrumbs. The submitted support report remains local.
