# AccessXI v2026.09.19

This update repairs Crawler's Nest terrain navigation and prevents unnamed quest
objects from resolving to unrelated zones. The installer retains Ashita 4.3.2.1
and the inventory, search, Treasure Pool and player trade readers from September 17.

## Crawler's Nest

The support report rejected an entrance-to-Exoray route because the generated
terrain separated the entrance hill from the interior. The legacy mesh fallback
returned only a destination point; rejecting that result was correct.

Zone 197 now generates candidate corridors at 0.25-yalm resolution, then checks
their ground support against the original collision triangles and applies the
current client's player contact calculations. A bounded local search can route
around a small cave-floor seam; longer rejected links are divided into checked
sections before that search. Candidate raster settings alone never authorize
a route. Other zones retain their existing terrain profiles and capsule checks.

The mathematical contact model was checked against the September retail client's
triangle, edge, and settlement routines using Ghidra and an isolated local probe.
The game binary and extracted machine code are not included in the project or
installer. All 20,201 comparison cases agreed on successful versus unsuccessful
settlement. Two corrected positions exceeded the strict 0.0002-yalm comparison
tolerance; the maximum difference was 0.008015 yalm. This is not a bit-exact port.
Eight additional client-derived floor/wall fixtures passed at 0.0001 tolerance.

The reader preserves the validated cave route instead of applying its generic
capsule-only shortcut smoother. Short cave corners use a smaller arrival
tolerance, and route progression is limited to the current and next segment so
a nearby later corridor cannot steal progress. These tests execute the actual
production reader functions and reproduce both defects before the fix.

Crawler's Nest planning runs on a background worker. The addon announces planning
and retains the request until completion. Changing destination, zone or player
position cancels the old request; its result cannot replace the new one. Existing
native exports and structure layouts retain ABI 3 compatibility. A library without
the added asynchronous exports reports an update requirement for this zone.
Planning restarts after more than 2 yalms of horizontal movement or 0.5 yalm of
vertical movement, so continued walking can delay completion. Replacing a request
briefly joins the canceled worker; cancellation is cooperative at contact checks.

One query shares a finite allowance of 1,000,000 floor casts/body resolutions.
The 82-camp sweep needed at most 482,855 units. Exact segment caching avoids
rechecking shared corridor sections. This is a work limit, not a time guarantee:
the most expensive measured routes took about 13 seconds in the background.

## Quest destinations

The reported quest, In Defiant Challenge (`quest:jeuno:128`), was present as an
active quest in the failing session. Its unscoped "click all three ???" step
incorrectly searched every unnamed object in the catalogue, offered West
Ronfaure, and committed that destination.

Unnamed objects now require a zone stated by their own step or action. Nation
defaults, the player's location and neighboring steps cannot establish that
identity. A step without sufficient location evidence still speaks its guide
instruction and offers no invented destination. Explicitly scoped objects,
including the existing Qufim example, continue to resolve.

Active quest listing still comes from the client's quest log and current-session
quest state. The regression includes Jeuno quest 128 and verifies that another
character's packet state cannot publish it. This update does not regenerate the
quest guide corpus or claim every collection substep has a precise destination.

## Validation and remaining limits

- Replayed the recorded entrance-to-Exoray query through the native library and
  its exported x86 DLL API: 61 waypoints, approximately 718 yalms.
- Found routes from that entrance to all 82 catalogued enemy camps, including all
  four Exoray camps. Also replayed the earlier logged player position and the
  reverse route from the reported camp to the entrance.
- Rejected a solid wall, a two-yalm ledge in both directions, and missing ground;
  retained the valid passage around the synthetic wall.
- Native context, ROM resolver, collision parser, world and existing multi-zone
  Recast regressions passed. The contact solver's 12 portable cases passed.
- The placeholder resolver passed 32 focused checks and 131 existing claims.
  Quest listing, guide and reader runtime integration tests passed.
- Lua 5.1 syntax, cave waypoint progression and collision-route smoothing checks
  passed on the production reader.
- The exported asynchronous x86 API passed pending/ready, replacement, cancellation,
  and caller-buffer checks. A difficult 85-point route completed alongside 398
  foreground collision sweeps; individual API calls and cancellation measured at
  most 1 millisecond in that replay. These timings apply to the test machine.
- Replayed the installed final DLL separately: the reported 61-point route and
  difficult 85-point route both passed, with 209 concurrent foreground sweeps.
  The installed Lua adapter also passed the asynchronous and stale-floor tests.

No in-game traversal was performed for this release. The route evidence is an
offline replay using the installed retail terrain and the reporter's coordinates.
Short contact links are limited to 0.75 horizontal and 2.0 vertical yalms, with
ground required within the bounded floor probe. Narrow depressions inside that
probe range remain an approximation of player movement. The client's small body
contact model replaces the full capsule check in Crawler's Nest only.

Automatic mission, quest, route and failure logging remains enabled. A new support
report can capture the next attempt without a manual route recording.
